About h5
********

``h5`` is a high-level C++20 interface to the HDF5 library, with a dict-like Python interface
built on top of it. It is a part of the `TRIQS <https://triqs.github.io>`_ project and is
developed at the Center for Computational Quantum Physics (CCQ) of the Flatiron Institute,
Simons Foundation.

Written and maintained by O. Parcollet, D. Simon, N. Wentzell and T. Hahn, with contributions
from the TRIQS community.
